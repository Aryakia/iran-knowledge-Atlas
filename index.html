Public Historical Modeling Showcase

Iran IQ & Knowledge Through Time

An exploratory System Dynamics model asking how knowledge accumulation and the societal conditions for realizing human capability may change under education, health, institutions, trade, migration, conflict, and cultural disruption over long historical periods.

Interpretation safeguard: this is a model, not a historical IQ dataset. Proxy-based model values are not measurements of inherited intelligence, ethnic ability, or a basis for ranking populations.

Research question

How does knowledge accumulate, persist, and get disrupted across centuries?

The model treats long-run development as a path-dependent process shaped by interacting educational, institutional, health, migration, trade, political, and conflict mechanisms.

Modeling rationale

History contains stocks, delays, and feedback.

Knowledge can accumulate slowly, disruption can have delayed effects, and institutions or education can reinforce future capability. System Dynamics makes those hypotheses explicit rather than leaving them as an informal narrative.

Core stocks
Knowledge Capital (K)A modeled stock representing accumulated societal knowledge and intellectual capacity. It can grow through creation, education, exchange, preservation, and continuity, while disruption can slow or erode accumulation.
Realized Knowledge Capability (G)A modeled realized knowledge capability shaped by enabling conditions such as health, education, institutions, and access to accumulated knowledge. It is not a biological intelligence measure.
Working model structure

Historical context + dynamic mechanisms

Political periods

Twenty modeled eras provide structured historical context across the long-run horizon.

Event layer

Fifty-five historical events support contextual interpretation of shocks, transitions, and disruptions.

Scholar layer

Thirty-six sourced scholar profiles provide an additional evidence layer around knowledge production.

Conflict mechanism

Persistent conflict pressure is separated from catastrophic state breach/invasion so not all war is modeled identically.

Geographic context

Regional illustrative extents and dated historical-map context support orientation without pretending maps are model measurements.

Transparent uncertainty

The working model documents assumptions, evidence classes, parameters, and limitations instead of hiding uncertainty.

Research status

Exploratory and uncalibrated.

Version 0.6 is intended for hypothesis development, structural reasoning, and scenario comparison. It is not intended for historical ranking, causal estimation from proxy values, or point prediction. Stronger claims would require calibration, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty testing, and expert review.

Disclosure boundary

Public structure, private working model.

The showcase excludes detailed equations, coefficients, working datasets, calibration experiments, unpublished coding decisions, private notes, credentials, and internal development files.
