Stage 9 · in development · Stage 9D next, currently gated

Empirical Calibration & Validation

Stage 9 is the transition from an exploratory, assumption-driven model toward a restricted modern model that can be evaluated against observed data. The purpose is not to fit the full 2,700-year reconstruction.

Stage 9 workflow · 9A–9C implemented · 9D–9E frameworks ready

Modern empirical pilot

9AAudit exact source series: definitions, units, coverage, missingness, breaks, and redistribution terms.
9BImplemented: role-specific observation model for latent stocks, drivers, and model flows with irregular observation masks.
9CImplemented: pre-estimation identifiability plan and local Jacobian screening; first-fit free subset reduced to two parameters, with baselineLoss profile-only.
9DFramework ready, empirical fit gated: training-only observation profiling and the predeclared two-parameter dynamic fit are implemented, but no canonical observations have been promoted.
9EFramework ready, validation gated: frozen-fit MAE/RMSE/bias scoring and persistence/linear-trend comparisons are implemented.
9FEstimate empirical parameter and observation uncertainty only where supported by the data.
9GApply a promotion gate determining which quantities may legitimately be described as empirically informed or calibrated.
Scientific boundary

What Stage 9 must not claim

A successful modern calibration would not make the historical Knowledge Capital or Realized Knowledge Capability trajectories observed quantities. It would provide evidence about a restricted modern subsystem and about selected mechanisms or parameter combinations.

Gate: Stage 9 is complete only when data quality, parameter identification, held-out validation, and interpretation criteria are all satisfied.

Stage 9B–9E · current implementation status

The empirical architecture now reaches calibration and validation—without pretending either has occurred.

Stage 9B · implemented

Observation model

Separate equations now map reviewed indicators to latent-stock, driver, or flow signals. Irregular observation years are supported without creating missing empirical values.

Stage 9C · implemented

Identifiability pre-screen

The provisional first-fit dynamic subset is reduced to creationScale and formationScale; baselineLoss remains profile-only after a strong local confounding signal.

Stage 9D · framework ready

Training calibration

A training-only objective and observation-parameter profiling framework are implemented. No empirical parameter fit has been run because canonical observations have not yet been promoted.

Stage 9E · framework ready

Held-out validation

Frozen-fit MAE/RMSE/bias scoring and persistence/linear-trend comparisons are implemented. Validation remains closed until a Stage 9D fit is frozen.

Current boundary: zero dynamic parameters are calibrated and zero held-out validation results exist. The next substantive step is reviewed canonical data promotion, followed by rerunning identifiability on the actual training design before opening Stage 9D.

Stage 10 · planned after Stage 9

Historical Evidence & Reconstruction Validation

Stage 10 returns to the long historical reconstruction only after the modern empirical pilot produces interpretable results. Modern evidence becomes a constraint on reasoning, not a shortcut to reconstructing history.

Historical validation program

Evidence, uncertainty, transferability

10AComplete exact provenance for historical events, periods, scholars, institutions, and geographic claims.
10BRecord date, geographic, source, and interpretive uncertainty explicitly.
10CTest which modern mechanisms appear transferable across historical contexts and which do not.
10DAvoid transporting fitted modern parameter magnitudes backward without independent historical justification.
10EPropagate historical-input and structural uncertainty through reconstructed trajectories.
10FCompare alternative historical codings and structural interpretations.
10GConduct historical-domain review and document responses before upgrading historical claims.
Sequencing rule

Stage 9 first

Stage 10 should not begin upgrading historical claims merely because software for Stage 9 exists. It should begin only when Stage 9 has produced interpretable validation evidence and a clear account of what was—and was not—identified by modern data.

Gate: historical claims remain assumption/reconstruction statements until evidence and uncertainty criteria are satisfied.
