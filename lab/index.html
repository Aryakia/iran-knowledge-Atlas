v0.8 research architecture

Three implementation stages now sit behind the atlas.

The simulator has grown from a scenario viewer into a broader research laboratory.

Stage 1

Numerical laboratory

Euler/RK4 comparison, finer time steps, exact event timing, clipping diagnostics, and reproducibility metadata.

Stage 2

Experiment workbench

Policy timelines, scheduled shocks, flow decomposition, saved-run comparison, and experiment bundles.

Stage 3

Robustness laboratory

Seeded parameter-assumption ensembles, percentile fan charts, threshold summaries, and Morris global screening.
