v0.8 research architecture

The simulator now has three research layers.

The public website exposes reviewed outputs and comparison tools. The full research environment includes additional numerical and robustness analysis that is not shipped as executable browser logic here.

Stage 1

Numerical laboratory

Euler/RK4 comparison, finer time steps, exact event timing, clipping diagnostics, and reproducibility metadata.

Stage 2

Experiment workbench

Policy timelines, scheduled shocks, flow decomposition, saved-run comparison, and experiment bundles.

Stage 3

Robustness laboratory

Seeded parameter-assumption ensembles, percentile fan charts, threshold summaries, and Morris global screening.

Public distribution rule: this website publishes selected results and generic presentation code, while detailed equations, coefficients, parameter ranges, and analysis implementation are not distributed in the browser package.
