v0.8 research architecture

Four implementation stages now sit behind the atlas.

The simulator has grown from a scenario viewer into a broader research laboratory.

Stage 1

Numerical laboratory

Euler/RK4 comparison, finer time steps, exact event timing, clipping diagnostics, and reproducibility metadata.

Stage 2

Experiment workbench

Policy timelines, scheduled shocks, flow decomposition, saved-run comparison, and experiment bundles.

Stage 3

Robustness laboratory

Seeded parameter-assumption ensembles, percentile fan charts, threshold summaries, and Morris global screening.

Stage 4

Endogenous recovery

An optional Recovery Capacity stock adds rebuilding and renewal feedbacks while preserving direct comparison with the baseline structure.

Next research stages

Stage 4 and the Stage 5–8 foundations are now implemented.

Stage 4 is implemented as an uncalibrated structural hypothesis. Stages 5–8 add empirical-pilot, migration/network, gateway-geography, and publication-readiness foundations without claiming calibration, activated unsupported feedbacks, or submission readiness.

Stage 4 · implemented

Endogenous recovery capacity

Implemented an optional Knowledge Infrastructure / Recovery Capacity stock so rebuilding and renewal can be explored endogenously and compared directly with the preserved baseline structure.

Stage 5 · foundation implemented

Modern empirical pilot

Observation equations, an explicit zero-record data container, leakage-safe train/validation preparation, and benchmark evaluation are implemented. Exact comparable source series still need review and ingestion before calibration starts.

Stage 6 · foundation implemented

Migration & knowledge networks

The research architecture now separates local skilled loss, destination relocation, diaspora exchange, and return/reconnection. The evidence-linked network channels remain inactive until observable data and defensible equations are available.

Stage 7 · foundation implemented

Gateway geography

Exchange opportunity and strategic exposure are now separated as research channels under a closed evidence gate. Historical GIS/network measures still need to be ingested before any exposure equation is activated.

Stage 8 · foundation implemented

Publication readiness

A machine-readable readiness matrix now tracks evidence, numerics, empirical validation, reproducibility, manuscript assets, and external review. Publication causal-loop and stock-flow figures are now complete; the submission gate remains explicitly closed while major blockers remain.
